About RoadStar

Specialist-managed. Built to last.

RoadStar is managed by Roadstar Specialty LLC, a program of NorthStar Specialty Group. Coverage is written on commercial auto and general liability forms tailored to towing and roadside exposures, supported by independent actuarial analysis, dedicated claims handling, and underwriting focused exclusively on this class of business.

The program is designed to give operators a stable, specialist home for their insurance — with the option to become a member-owner as the program matures.
